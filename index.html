How I Think

My Performance Marketing Philosophy

01
Validate Before Spending
Organic signals tell you what resonates before you commit budget. I test content demand before scaling with paid ads — this protects budget and improves creative performance from launch.
02
Structure Protects ROAS
ROAS isn't maintained by luck — it's maintained by clean account architecture, proper funnel stages, and disciplined campaign management. I build for stability, not just early wins.
03
Creatives Do the Targeting
With Meta's Andromeda updates, over-targeting hurts more than it helps. I let strong creatives attract the right audience — focusing on hooks, angles, and formats rather than narrow interest stacking.
04
Data + Decision Logic
Numbers only matter if they lead to clear decisions. I review performance daily — not to produce reports, but to act: pause what isn't working, scale what is, and understand why.
